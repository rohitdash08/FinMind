- Category overspend detection: flag categories > 40% of total spending

- Duplicate prevention with opportunity upsert
- Opportunity lifecycle: active → dismissed or acted
- Savings summary with totals, acted vs pending, by-type breakdown
- Configurable analysis period and detection thresholds
- Sorted by potential savings (highest first)
