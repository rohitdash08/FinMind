
# Guided Monthly Financial Review

This 5-step guide will help you review your monthly spending and improve your financial habits using FinMind.

## Step 1: Export Your Data
Before analyzing your spending, export your financial data from FinMind.

1. **Log in** to your FinMind account
2. Navigate to the **Reports** section
3. Select **"Export Data"** and choose your preferred format (CSV or JSON)
4. Save the file to your computer for analysis

## Step 2: Categorize Your Spending
Organize your expenses into meaningful categories to understand your spending patterns.

1. Open your exported data file
2. Use FinMind's **Categorization Tool** to automatically assign transactions:
   - Housing (rent/mortgage)
   - Utilities (electricity, water, internet)
   - Transportation (car payments, gas, public transit)
   - Food (groceries, dining out)
   - Healthcare
   - Personal (clothing, entertainment)
   - Savings/Investments
   - Debt Payments
   - Other

3. Review and adjust any automatically categorized items that don't match your expectations

## Step 3: Compare to Your Budget
Review your spending against your monthly budget plan.

1. Open your **Budget Plan** in FinMind
2. Compare actual spending vs. budgeted amounts for each category
3. Identify areas where you spent more than planned
4. Note categories where you spent less than budgeted

## Step 4: Identify Spending Leaks
Find areas where you might be overspending without realizing it.

1. Look for categories with significant variances from your budget
2. Review recent transactions in high-spending categories
3. Identify patterns like:
   - Unplanned purchases
   - Recurring subscriptions you forgot about
   - Impulse buys
   - High-cost services you could reduce

4. Note specific transactions that seem unusual or excessive

## Step 5: Set Goals for Next Month
Use your findings to create a better financial plan.

1. Adjust your budget based on what you learned:
   - Increase allocations for important needs
   - Reduce spending in areas where you overspent
   - Set aside more for savings or debt repayment

2. Set specific goals for next month:
   - "Save $X for emergency fund"
   - "Reduce dining out expenses by $Y"
   - "Pay off $Z of credit card debt"

3. Create action items to help you stay on track:
   - Set up budget alerts in FinMind
   - Schedule regular spending reviews
   - Plan for upcoming expenses

## Pro Tips
- Use FinMind's **visualization tools** to see spending patterns at a glance
- Compare this month's spending to previous months to identify trends
- Celebrate your successes and learn from areas where you struggled
- Adjust your financial plan as needed throughout the month

## FinMind-Specific Features
- Use the **"Spending Insights"** dashboard to get automated recommendations
- Set up **"Smart Alerts"** for categories that need attention
- Use the **"Goal Tracker"** to monitor your progress toward financial objectives
